How this report makes decisions

Resale candidates must be commodities with at least 20 tracker-confirmed units over 24 hours, a confirmation share of 30% or more, 20 comparable hours, 18 observed hourly asks, a tracked sale within 6 hours, and no more than two days of stock relative to confirmed daily demand. Hourly asks must stay within a 2× range; drops beyond 50% of the price anchor are watch-only.

The price anchor is the lowest of the 24-hour inferred sale average, last tracked sale price, and median hourly ask. The target is 90% of that anchor, capped below the next listing level. Purchase quantities use the stock within 5% of the ask as an upper bound, with a buy ceiling shown on each card. Open the order book to check actual depth.

Reports older than two hours produce no purchase plan. Market confirmation unlocks the plan. Character skill values are saved and displayed on material matches; they do not yet establish recipe knowledge or crafting eligibility. Gathering time and materials are never treated as free.
